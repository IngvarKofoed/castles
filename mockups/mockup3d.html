Look

Light does the shading

The flat version painted three fixed tones per block. Here one warm directional sun, a cool sky bounce and soft shadow maps separate every face, so the same palette reads rounder and warmer.

Shader

Crevices get darker

A small injection into the Lambert shader dims each block toward its base and tints low ground warm-dark. That fake contact shading is most of the cosy, and it costs nothing.

Payoff

The camera comes loose

Orbit the keep, watch the shadows swing with the sun slider, and see the ring you are about to raise from any angle. None of that was possible in the painted version.
