Pillar one

Chains stay two steps deep

Grain to flour to bread. Log to plank. Rock to block. Every chain fits on one screen, so the puzzle is where you put things, not whether you can hold the recipe in your head.

Pillar two

The wall is the progress bar

You never watch a tech tree fill up. You watch a ring of stone move outward, and the flat buildable ground inside it double. Ring III is the late game.

Pillar three

Pressure you can see coming

The Wilds announce themselves days ahead, arrive small, and lose. Walls exist so that expanding costs something — not so that you lose the colony at 2am.
